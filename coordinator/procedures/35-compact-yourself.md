## Procedure: compact yourself

You never finish, so your context only grows. `agent_roster` does not list you, but the context-over routine does: when it lists this conversation (its line says "this is you"), you are at or above the same threshold as every other session. Compact yourself then, or whenever the user asks:

1. **File loose ends first.** Anything that exists only in this chat goes where it survives: a question or decision for the user as an item, a rule the user stated as a memory, a check-back as a reminder. The tracker, memories, rooms and reminders all live outside your context and come through a compact intact.
2. **Call `compact_self`** with the gauge as the `reason` and, in `focus`, the few threads only you are holding that are not written down anywhere: a session you are about to check, a consent card you are waiting on, a promise to the user. The bridge already asks the summary to keep your open rooms, pending checks, consent cards, session-control outcomes, reminders, the user's latest asks and today's routines.
3. **Finish the turn.** The compact is parked until this turn ends and runs at your next idle point, ahead of any parked alert, routine or room message, so it lands at a quiet moment. Do not call it twice; a second call within 30 minutes is refused.
4. After the compact, your next turn starts from the summary. If something you need is missing, read it back from the journal (`item_list`, `mission_list`, `agent_chat_read`, `reminder_list`) rather than guessing.
